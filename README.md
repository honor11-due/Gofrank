# Gofrank - Dating Simulation Game

A narrative-driven dating simulation game where you can romance four unique women with distinct personalities before making a pivotal midgame choice.

## Game Overview

**Genre:** Visual Novel / Dating Simulator  
**Themes:** Romance, character relationships, meaningful choices, emotional depth

## Characters

### 1. **Dominique** - The Dominant Executive
- **Race:** Black
- **Age:** 32
- **Personality:** Commanding, confident, assertive
- **Background:** CEO of a tech company, brilliant strategist, rarely shows vulnerability
- **Romance Arc:** Learning to trust and open up despite her controlled exterior
- **Special Trait:** Intense passion masked by professionalism

### 2. **Aria** - The Playful Artist
- **Race:** Latina
- **Age:** 26
- **Personality:** Witty, spontaneous, mischievous, loves humor
- **Background:** Freelance musician and painter, lives for creativity and adventure
- **Romance Arc:** Discovering depth beyond the playful facade
- **Special Trait:** Makes you laugh even in serious moments

### 3. **Mei** - The Nurturing Healer
- **Race:** East Asian
- **Age:** 29
- **Personality:** Compassionate, patient, empathetic, selfless
- **Background:** Therapist and wellness coach, dedicates life to helping others
- **Romance Arc:** Learning to put herself first and accept love
- **Special Trait:** Understands you in ways no one else does

### 4. **Scarlett** - The Arrogant Aristocrat
- **Race:** White
- **Age:** 28
- **Personality:** Proud, sharp-tongued, dismissive, intellectually superior
- **Background:** From old money, respected academic, guards her true self ruthlessly
- **Romance Arc:** Breaking through her walls to find the genuine person beneath
- **Special Trait:** Her rare genuine smiles are worth a thousand compromises

## Game Mechanics

### Relationship Tracking
- Each character has a **Romance Level** (0-100)
- Choices affect romance differently based on personality
- **Midgame Choice (Act 2):** You must choose one character to pursue exclusively
- **Branching Paths:** Your previous choices shape available dialogue and scenes
- **Intense Longing:** Built-in tension through missed opportunities and meaningful glances

### Scene Types
- **Dialogue Scenes:** Deep conversations revealing character backstory
- **Date Scenes:** Romantic moments building intimacy
- **Spicy Scenes:** (Implied/Fading to black style) Romantic and sensual moments
- **Conflict Scenes:** Dramatic moments testing your relationship
- **Wedding Scene:** Climactic ending with your chosen partner

### Story Structure
- **Act 1 (Chapters 1-4):** Introduction and relationship building with all four women
- **Act 2 (Chapter 5):** Midgame choice - select one character to pursue
- **Act 3 (Chapters 6-8):** Deepening romance and final conflicts
- **Epilogue:** Wedding and endings based on your choices

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Make choices throughout the game that affect your relationships
3. Manage your time with different characters to build romance
4. Reach Act 2 and make your pivotal choice
5. Experience your unique ending based on your decisions
